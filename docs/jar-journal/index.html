Jar Journal Support

Help for the Jar Journal app for iPhone

Jar Journal is a journal for your concentrate jars, for adults 21 and over where cannabis is legal. Need help? Email manny@brickandsignal.com and we'll get back to you.

Do I need an account?

No. Every journal feature works without one. Sign in with Apple only if you want to share your ratings and photos.

Where is my data?

Your jars, dab log and notes stay on your phone. Only what you choose to share is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

Report, hide or block

Every shared photo and product name has Report and Hide. You can also block a user so their photos never show again.

Delete your account

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. Your shared ratings, photos and profile are removed right away.
